The self-propelled scissor lift has an automatic walking function, with an integrated design and a built-in battery power supply. Hydraulic scissor lifts can lift and lower freely under different working conditions without the need for external power or external traction. The walking and turning of the battery powered scissor lift can be completed by only one person. Load capacity 230–450 kg, max working height 8–15.8 m.

  • Automatic walking function — drive and steer from the platform by one person

  • 90 cm extended deck for extra reach over ground obstacles
  • Pothole protection, tilt alarm, overload protection and emergency descent
  • Non-marking solid tires for indoor floors
  • CE and ISO certified; 2-year structure warranty

Product Description

The self-propelled electric scissor lift is ideal for modern enterprises that need efficient, safe and emission-free vertical access. One operator can drive, steer and lift from the platform, making it a true one-person aerial work solution. The reinforced Q345 steel scissor pack provides rock-solid stability, while the 90 cm extension deck lets you reach over machines, racking and other ground-level obstacles.
