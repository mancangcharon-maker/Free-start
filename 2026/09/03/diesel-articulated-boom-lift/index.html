Product Description

Diesel articulated boom lifts are ideal for outdoor work on large work-sites. They are widely used in warehousing, industrial applications, pipework, electrical installations, fire protection, air conditioning, aeronautical and oil industry work, building maintenance and tree surgery. The 4×4 drive system and large rough-terrain tires allow operation on uneven ground, while the articulated boom reaches over obstacles for precise positioning.
