Product Description

Electric articulating boom lifts are revolutionizing vertical access with unmatched flexibility, eco-friendly operation and precision. Designed for complex tasks in confined spaces, these machines combine zero emissions, 360° articulation and quiet operation to outperform traditional diesel-powered lifts. The power of self-propelled articulated boom platforms is divided into diesel engine models and DC motor models. The lifting height ranges from 10 m to 32 m, and all electric models can walk at full height, extend the boom for lifting and rotate 360°.
