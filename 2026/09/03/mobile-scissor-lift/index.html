Product Description

Mobile scissor lift is widely used for station, dock, bridge, hall, workshop indoor and outdoor machinery installation, equipment repair and building maintenance. Its scissor-type mechanical structure gives the platform higher stability, and the spacious platform allows several people to work at the same time. Standard models cover platform heights from 4 m to 18 m with load capacities from 300 kg to 500 kg.
