About SafeLift

Engineering Reliable Lifts Since 2008

A China-based manufacturer of aerial work platforms and material handling equipment, exporting to 80+ countries with full CE certification.

20,000 m² Production Facility — Jiyang, Shandong

From Our Factory Floor to Your Job Site

SafeLift is located in Jiyang District, Jinan City — China's largest aerial work platform manufacturing hub. With over 15 years of experience, we specialize in the design, production and export of scissor lifts, boom lifts, dock ramps and material handling equipment.

Our 20,000 m² facility houses robotic welding lines, CNC cutting, electrostatic painting and final assembly stations. Every unit undergoes a 48-hour load test and full inspection before shipment. We hold full-machine CE certification by TUV, ensuring smooth customs clearance across the European Union and beyond.

We work directly with rental companies, contractors, distributors and end-users worldwide — no trading company, no middleman markup. Our English-speaking sales team provides quotations within 24 hours and supports you from specification selection through after-sales service.
